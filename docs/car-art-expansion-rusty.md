# Rusty expansion artwork

## Painted-panel condition constraint (2026-09-30)

Rusty vehicles retain their original body paint. The paint may be faded, chipped, and scratched, with localized heavy flaking corrosion at arches, rocker panels, seams, and lower edges; small pierced rust holes, dents, tired trim, and aged glass are allowed. Never strip a whole body panel, door, or fender to bare steel or replace it with an entirely bare/mismatched door. Preserve recognizability, original side-profile framing, transparent alpha, and two intact wheels. This user-confirmed constraint supersedes earlier rusty prompts that requested large exposed panels or a mismatched replacement door.

# Car art club sprite references

Generated with the built-in OpenAI imagegen tool. Each final PNG is a standalone RGBA cutout at 2172 × 724 pixels. The real vehicle photo listed for each car was downloaded and visually inspected, then passed to imagegen as a pixel reference. Outputs were inspected and horizontally flipped where needed to make every car face right. `src/assets/cars/club-metadata.json` records sprite bounds, wheel centers and radii, paint colors, and reference URLs in original-image pixel coordinates.
